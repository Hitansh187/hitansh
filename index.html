02 · THE GAME

My heart is on the cricket field. 🏏

🏏 Why Cricket?

Cricket is not just about hitting a ball. It is about patience, decision-making, discipline, teamwork, pressure and confidence. Every delivery is another opportunity.

👑 My Inspiration

Virat Kohli is a major inspiration to me. His intensity, discipline, confidence and commitment to improvement show how powerful consistent effort can be.

🔥 The Dream

I want to keep progressing through competitive cricket and discover how far my skills, training and determination can take me. The dream is big. So is the work required.

One bad ball does not decide an innings. One bad match does not decide a career. Reset. Learn. Face the next ball. That mentality works outside cricket too.
